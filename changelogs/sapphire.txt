installion guide

Download orange fox recovery or twrp
Flash Recovery 
Flash respective FW 
Flash Rom+ reflash orangefox/twrp
Reboot to recovery 
Format data.
Reboot

if rom don't have gapps
flash ro2rw
Reboot to recovery 
flash gapps 
Format data
Reboot
